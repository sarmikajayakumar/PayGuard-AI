🚀 PAYGUARD AI – AI-Powered Digital Payment Fraud Detection & Prevention

PAYGUARD AI is an intelligent digital payment safety platform designed to detect and prevent online payment scams, including UPI fraud, phishing messages, fake payment screenshots, suspicious payment requests, and OTP-related scams.

Using Artificial Intelligence and rule-based fraud detection, PAYGUARD AI analyzes suspicious messages, identifies potential fraud indicators, generates risk scores, and provides actionable safety recommendations.

✨ Key Features:
- AI-powered scam message detection
- UPI payment request risk analysis
- Fake payment screenshot analysis using OCR
- Explainable fraud risk scoring
- AI-powered safety assistant
- Tamil and English language support
- Family safety awareness features
- Cybercrime reporting guidance

🛠️ Tech Stack: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Python/FastAPI, Google Gemini API, Supabase, and Tesseract.js.

🎯 Our Mission: To make digital payments safer by helping people identify suspicious activities before they lose their money.
